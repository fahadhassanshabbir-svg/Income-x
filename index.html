04   TRUCK DISPATCHING

View the guide →
🚛

BEGINNER ROADMAP

  1. Understand the trucking industry
  2. Learn dispatcher terminology
  3. Know truck and trailer types
  4. Learn load booking basics
  5. Practice broker communication
  6. Prepare for dispatcher jobs
DISPATCHING GUIDE

What you should learn.

Industry basics

Dispatcher, carrier, broker, shipper and driver roles.

Truck types

Dry van, reefer, flatbed, box truck and basic equipment knowledge.

Important terms

Rate confirmation, BOL, POD, detention, layover and deadhead.

Load booking

Find a load → call the broker → negotiate → book → dispatch.

Communication

Professional English, negotiation, follow-ups and problem solving.

Career preparation

Build your resume and practice common dispatcher interview questions.
